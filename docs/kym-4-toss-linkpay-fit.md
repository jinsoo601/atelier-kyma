## Decision

**LinkPay remains a conditional v1 candidate, not yet cleared for live checkout.** It supports a hosted payment link and a product with a price, quantity, delivery information, and return policy. A product can be edited, and orders can be inspected in the Toss merchant dashboard. For the current ₩2m pilot, the gallery needs an approved merchant/LinkPay agreement, actual fees and enabled payment methods, price editing, and quantity-one behavior during concurrent or unpaid payment attempts. KYM-5 tests transaction behavior after access exists. The ₩12m work has additional high-value approval and settlement questions.

## Why LinkPay was considered, and alternatives

LinkPay was an **initial low-implementation candidate, not the only valid Toss product or a completed product comparison**. The project has few purchases, one-of-one stock, and a desire to avoid building an editor or order-management backend. [LinkPay's guide](https://docs.tosspayments.com/guides/v2/linkpay) says the gallery can create a product and hosted checkout link in the merchant dashboard, with a stock feature that limits the number of payments. Its [product API](https://docs.tosspayments.com/reference/linkpay) also exposes amount, stock, sold-out status, and edits. These features match the proposed owner workflow, but concurrent and pending-payment semantics remain unverified. An additional LinkPay agreement and its actual terms are also unverified.

| Toss route | Fit for this pilot | Work the gallery/site would own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkPay** | Potentially simplest self-service checkout for a few fixed-price works, if approved and stock-one behavior passes testing. | Keep the artwork page's displayed price/availability in sync with the hosted product; handle gallery sales and fulfillment. |
| **결제창형 (payment window)** | Valid fallback if LinkPay is unavailable or its terms are poor. Toss calls it suitable for a fast integration with limited UI change. | Build and maintain the site's order form, order IDs, server-side payment confirmation, order record, and atomic stock reservation/sold-out handling. [Official flow](https://docs.tosspayments.com/guides/v2/payment-widget/integration-window). |
| **주문서형 (embedded order-form UI)** | Valid when a more integrated checkout experience matters; not needed to prove the first one-work sale. | Similar server/order/stock work, plus render Toss payment UI on the gallery's order page. [Official comparison](https://docs.tosspayments.com/guides/v2/payment-widget). |
| **자체창형 / 브랜드페이 / ARS / 해외결제** | No clear advantage for a Korean, low-volume, buyer-initiated one-time purchase. | More custom UI or a different use case; [ARS is phone-oriented and API-only](https://docs.tosspayments.com/guides/v2/ars-payment). |

The payment-window and order-form products supply payment UI; they do not by themselves make a unique gallery object unavailable while two buyers attempt checkout. That stock/order design would be ours. This is an implementation inference from their [documented order and approval flow](https://docs.tosspayments.com/guides/v2/get-started/payment-flow). The choice should be revisited when Toss answers whether LinkPay can be contracted alone, what it costs, and how its quantity-one stock works. If those fail, compare **결제창형** against keeping the pilot on the on-site inquiry path. This evaluation is limited to Toss products; it is not a complete survey of Korean payment providers.

The gallery will also offer an **on-site Korean purchase inquiry form**. It is the primary path for works or buyers that cannot use approved checkout, and a secondary path for delivery or payment questions when checkout is available. The form sends a lead to the owner; it does not reserve stock or take payment. The buyer and owner workflow, provisional form-delivery provider, and related implementation tasks are in [the manual inquiry plan](./kym-4-manual-inquiry-fallback.md). Do not interpret the published ₩10m real-time-transfer daily limit as a general per-work LinkPay cap.

## Price and stock operating model to test

For the first version, use a **₩2,000,000**, **quantity 1** test product for **청화백금닷 달항아리** once the account permits LinkPay testing. Create or expose a live product only after merchant and owner launch approval. The artwork page can link to its hosted checkout once approved. Treat Toss as the authority for the amount actually charged and stock available. KYM-7 must choose how the website reads or synchronizes the price so that a later dashboard edit cannot leave the website showing an old amount. KYM-5 must test dashboard and API editing; the public API supports changing `amount`, but dashboard price and sold-out controls need confirmation. An in-gallery sale requires the owner to stop payment availability immediately and then update the website.

The LinkPay API documents one delivery fee and period for a product. It does not establish a built-in choice between nationwide mailing and free/paid Seoul hand delivery. If both are offered, either include domestic fulfillment in one displayed total and arrange the method after purchase, or verify an approved selectable flow before launch. Do not introduce a surprise delivery charge after payment.

The current [public contact page](https://www.atelierkyma.com/contact) shows a phone number and address but no visible business-registration or communications-sales registration details in the page content reviewed. Toss's [card-review guidance](https://www.tosspayments.com/blog/articles/21130) asks for these merchant details, a real sale-ready product, matching display and charge amounts, and delivery/return information. Verify the gallery's registration details and add the required disclosures during the rebuild before underwriting.

## Questions sent to Toss for the separate ₩12m work

1. Will Toss approve **LinkPay for a Korean art gallery selling a single original ceramic artwork for ₩12,000,000**, and which payment methods will be enabled at that amount? Is there an item, transaction, daily, or monthly card/LinkPay limit beyond the buyer's card limit?
2. Does this product trigger additional card-company screening, and what listing, business-registration, communications-sales registration, delivery/returns, or provenance information does Toss require? What is the realistic onboarding schedule for LinkPay and the enabled methods?
3. What monthly settlement limit would the gallery receive? For one or more ₩12m sales, what surety bond/collateral, premium, reserve, or delayed settlement applies? What are the actual settlement dates by payment method?
4. What are the exact LinkPay fees for domestic cards, easy pay, and virtual accounts, including VAT, annual/setup charges, canceled/refunded transactions, and any extra charge for high-value goods?
5. With stock set to **1**, at what stage is stock reserved or reduced for card checkout and for a virtual account issued but not paid? Can two shoppers ever both pay? What happens when a buyer abandons checkout, a virtual account expires, or a payment is refunded?
6. Can the gallery owner edit price and mark a product sold out from the dashboard without a developer? What exact dashboard control disables payment immediately after an in-gallery sale? Does changing a price preserve the existing link? Are paid/created orders locked to the old amount?
7. What order alerts does the owner receive by default? Can refunds and full cancellation be completed in the dashboard for every enabled payment method? For a virtual-account refund, how is the buyer's refund account collected?

## Merchant handoff

Toss's [LinkPay page](https://docs.tosspayments.com/guides/v2/linkpay) links to a [separate PG/LinkPay consultation form](https://builder.tosspayments.com/to/efa977a3cd8b4f61b31c46ade8d9e2c9); it also lists **1544-7772** and **support@tosspayments.com**. The form asks for a contact name, email, phone, registered company name and number, website, inquiry type, and message. The published LinkPay guide says an **additional contract** is required, but does **not establish that a standard 일반 결제 contract must be signed first**. Correct the earlier assumption: the gallery should ask Toss whether LinkPay can be contracted on its own, and what standard PG agreement, if any, is required before accepting contract fees or terms. With no existing PG contract, **PG 신규 도입 상담** is a reasonable form category; the message must explicitly ask about LinkPay-only eligibility. This is form-routing guidance, not a confirmation of Toss's internal routing. The exact sent texts and provider receipt have not been independently verified. Record any reply and merchant-specific terms in KYM-4 before deciding whether to enable checkout.
